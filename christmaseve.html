God With Us

Experience the profound peace and community of the Savior's birth. Join us for a full day of spiritual renewal and festive fellowship.

Celebration Highlights

10:00 AM

Christmas Eve Service

A serene morning of scripture and hymns. We gather to reflect on the miraculous birth through contemplative readings and choral arrangements that echo the celestial host.

01:30 PM

Community Feast

Shared Luncheon at the Grace Courtyard. A time for family, friends, and newcomers to break bread together in the spirit of communal love and festive abundance.

04:00 PM

Carol Night

Joyful Melodies under the open sky. Join the RCK Worship Ministry for an evening of classic carols and contemporary praise as we welcome the night.

06:00 PM

Children's Nativity Play

The story of old told by the young. A heartwarming performance by our Sunday School ministry, bringing the manger scene to life through the eyes of innocence.
