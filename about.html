About Us

A Vibrant Community

R.C.K Nairobi Church is a vibrant and welcoming community of believers dedicated to worship, fellowship, and serving others. Our church serves as a sanctuary where people from all walks of life can come together to grow in their faith, find support in times of need, and celebrate the transformative power of God's love.

"The story of R.C.K Nairobi Church is one of humble beginnings and extraordinary faithfulness."

It all started in 1990, in the intimate setting of a living room, where Rev. Patrick Mrabu and a small, dedicated group of faithful individuals gathered with a shared vision of creating a space for spiritual growth and community impact. What began as a small gathering fueled by prayer and a passion for the Gospel has since blossomed into a thriving congregation. Over the years, we have grown and evolved, eventually becoming a proud branch of R.C.K Nairobi.

The Founding Journey

Rev. Patrick Mrabu's vision wasn't just about a building; it was about building people. From that first living room meeting to our current facility, every step has been marked by faith.

As a branch of R.C.K Nairobi, we carry forward a heritage of excellence and spiritual depth, serving the Mathare community with unwavering commitment.
