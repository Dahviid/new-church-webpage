About Us

A History of Faith & Resilience

Revival Churches of Kenya (R.C.K) began as a humble vision to spread the transformative power of the Gospel across the nation. With our headquarters firmly established in the heart of Nakuru, we have grown into a spiritual lighthouse for thousands.

The Nairobi branch was founded in 1990, during a season of great spiritual awakening. Over the past three decades, we have stood as a pillar of hope in our community, dedicated to bringing the light of Christ to every home and heart in Mathare and beyond.

We are more than just a building; we are a family. Under the divine guidance of our leader Rev.Patrick Mrabu and Mrs.Jane Mrabu, we continue to strive for spiritual excellence and community development, ensuring that the legacy of faith continues for generations to come.
