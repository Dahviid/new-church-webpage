OUR MINISTRIES

"For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many." — Mark 10:45

Youth Ministry

Empowering the next generation to know Christ, live with purpose, and impact the world. We provide a safe and inspiring environment where young people can grow spiritually, connect with others, and serve God with confidence.

join us

Choir

Music is a powerful expression of faith, and our Choir Ministry seeks to honor God through every note sung. We are committed to leading meaningful worship experiences that inspire, encourage, and draw people into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

Join us every Saturday from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at our church for a worship experience as we serve God in our voices.

Join the Voice

Children's Sunday School

Our Sunday School Ministry is dedicated to nurturing children in the knowledge and love of God. Through age-appropriate Bible lessons, worship, activities, and prayer, we help young hearts grow in faith and develop a strong foundation in God's Word.

Sunday School classes are divided in respect to age for appropriate nurturing of the coming generation. Every sunday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, bring your child and let them learn from our abled teachers on the righteous way of life.

Mathew 19:14 Jesus said, "Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these."

Women's Fellowship

Our Women's Fellowship Ministry is a community of women united in faith, friendship, and service. Through prayer, Bible study, mentorship, and fellowship, we encourage one another to grow spiritually, strengthen our families, and live out God's purpose in our daily lives.

And So Much More...

Beyond our main ministries, we have active teams Open Air, Men's Bible Study, door to door fellowship, and Technical Production. There is a place for everyone at R.C.K Mathare.
